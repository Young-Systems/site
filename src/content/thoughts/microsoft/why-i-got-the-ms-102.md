That is a great question. The reason I went for a cert that was retiring is not only due to the level of the cert (while it was a good portion of the why), rather it was due to what it stood for and the content within.

As Microsoft administrators, we deal with a **lot** of weird decisions made by Microsoft. One of which is the decision to retire this certification and to replace it with an [associate level AI cert](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/ab-650/).

Obviously AI is a big part of Microsoft now and I'm sure that there's *some* good information in it, but I certainly felt that this was a step in the wrong direction. 

The current MS-102 exam had a LOT of good information that I wouldn't otherwise know on platforms such as **Microsoft Defender** and **Purview** outside of specialized security training, solidifying topics learned on the SC-300 certification, and overall great guidance on managing a Microsoft tenant.

#### Will I get the new cert?
Maybe. It really depends on whether I encounter a new roadblock or find that the information is useful in my work.

In the end, I want to ensure that the work I do is backed up by the knowledge I gain, and to establish trust by certifying that knowledge.
